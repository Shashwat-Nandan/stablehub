Ethereum remains the dominant Layer-1 blockchain for stablecoin issuance and deployment. With over $100 billion in stablecoin market cap, including USDT, USDC, and DAI, Ethereum's smart contract capabilities have made it the go-to platform for both centralized and decentralized stablecoins.

## Key Features

- ERC-20 token standard enabling seamless integration
- Robust DeFi ecosystem for stablecoin utility
- Post-merge energy efficiency improvements
- Strong security through extensive validator network

**Notable Stablecoins:** USDT, USDC, DAI, FRAX, LUSD
